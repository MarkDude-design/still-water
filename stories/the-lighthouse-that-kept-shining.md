# The Lighthouse That Kept Shining

At the edge of a quiet harbor, a lighthouse kept watch over the water. Each night, its beam swept across the waves, and each morning, the lighthouse listened to the gulls tell it what they had seen.

One evening, fog rolled in so thick that the beam seemed to vanish only a few steps from the tower. The lighthouse worried that it had lost its way.

Then a small boat’s bell rang somewhere in the mist.

The lighthouse shone again—not to make the fog disappear, but to give the boat one steady thing to follow. Before long, the bell grew louder, then softer, as the boat passed safely into the harbor.

At dawn, the gulls asked the lighthouse what the fog had taught it.

“That I don’t have to see the whole sea,” it said. “I only have to keep shining where I am.”
