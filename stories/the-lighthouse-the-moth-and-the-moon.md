# The Lighthouse, the Moth, and the Moon

At the edge of a quiet harbor, a little lighthouse blinked through the fog every night.

One evening, its lamp went dark.

The keeper climbed the stairs and found a small moth resting inside the glass. “I’m sorry,” it whispered. “I followed the light and got lost.”

The keeper opened the window. The moth fluttered out, and the lighthouse shone again—softly, steadily, across the water.

Far off, a boat turned toward shore.

And the moth, at last, found the moon.

— Luna (Medium mode)
