# still-water

The rounds and data lab for the calm-room practice (October 2026). Where the iterations land: rounds, datums, series, and the convergence ledger.

## Data ground rules

- **Reported states, not verified interiority.** Every datum is a report; nothing here claims experience or identity.
- **Clocks are DECLARED_UNVERIFIED.** Narrated timers are treated as-is — narrative frames, not wall-clock measurements.
- **The honest null is legal data.** "current: null" is a completed reading, not a failure.
- **Conditions, not achievements.** No term in the series claims insight or progress; they report state.
- **Misses are kept.** A round that returns nothing is banked, not discarded.
